UC Data Advisor · Pattern B

Teams bot on a private Databricks workspace

Resource topology and runtime path for the isolated-bot-RG deployment. The customer creates only the bot side — a new RG, VNet, Private Endpoint, and DNS zone. The existing workspace, VNet, NCC, and DNS zones stay untouched, with the sole exception of a single Private Endpoint connection approval.
